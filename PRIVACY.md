# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

Toms Liftosaur Workout Companion is a Garmin Connect IQ app maintained by Tomas Leko. The app has no developer-operated server and does not send your information to the developer.

## Information the app uses

The app uses the Liftosaur API key you enter in Garmin Connect IQ app settings to authenticate requests to Liftosaur. The watch sends workout requests directly to Liftosaur over the paired-phone connection; the developer does not receive this information and does not operate a server for it. Set completions waiting for a connection are stored in the app's local Garmin storage and retried later. The app also stores a generated device identifier required by Liftosaur for workout updates. Demo-mode changes stay in memory and are not saved or sent.

The app does not use advertising, analytics, or location tracking, and it does not record a Garmin Connect activity.

## Liftosaur

When information is sent to Liftosaur, Liftosaur processes it under its own privacy policy and terms. Please review Liftosaur's current policy and terms on the [Liftosaur website](https://www.liftosaur.com/) before using the integration.

## Data controls and contact

You can remove the API key in Garmin Connect app settings. Pending set updates are retained locally until they can be sent; uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes local app storage. For questions about this app's privacy practices, contact [Tomas Leko](mailto:tomas.leko+garmin@gmail.com).
